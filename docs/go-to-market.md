# Design-partner and commercial playbook

## Qualification

A design partner should have:

- at least $50,000 in monthly model spend
- a support, back-office, or operational agent with repeatable workflows
- one incumbent and at least one credible lower-cost candidate
- a trustworthy final outcome available for control and treatment
- enough traffic to preserve a randomized incumbent holdout
- an engineering owner who can add routing metadata and outcome events

Decline prospects whose primary need is unified billing, generic logs, or
provider failover.

## Four-week Routing Audit — $10,000

The fee is credited to an annual agreement.

1. Instrument the incumbent and validate outcome coverage.
2. Replay historical traffic and establish the incumbent cost/outcome baseline.
3. Shadow candidates within a customer-approved dollar budget.
4. Label paired outputs with executable checks, operations review, or existing
   workflow outcomes.
5. Deliver the Pareto report, viable model pool, integration estimate, and
   controlled-canary recommendation.

Required deliverables:

- labeled evaluation population and exclusions
- cost model using the customer’s negotiated prices
- outcome definitions and lag distribution
- model capability and privacy matrix
- estimated savings with confidence limits
- go/no-go criteria for the live pilot

## Production pricing

- $3,000 monthly minimum including five million routing decisions
- $0.40 per thousand decisions above the included volume
- no inference markup
- first-year service credits if fees exceed 20% of statistically verified
  gross savings
- VPC/local data plane, SSO, audit export, and contractual SLA from $50,000 per
  year

## Six-month commercial milestones

- five qualified design partners
- three paid production pilots
- two annual conversions
- two permissioned, reproducible case studies

Stop or change the wedge if fewer than five buyers will share outcomes, paired
evaluation shows less than 20% gross opportunity, or instrumentation repeatedly
takes more than one engineering week.
